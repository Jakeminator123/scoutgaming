The fixed seated preview now has one photographic room plate, the real 3D table/dealer, and a floor shadow receiver. Portrait camera movement preserves the seated viewing angle. The editable combined Blender project includes the same room reference and camera; geometric room source is retained. The plate is not walkable geometry or a full 3D reconstruction.

At 390×844, controls remain in bounds, but the dealer/cards are too small and the portrait room crop exaggerates background table scale. This phone composition is not accepted. Physical iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and current desktop Safari are still unverified.

Face/hair/clothing remain visibly computer generated. Hair coverage, material detail, rear garment gaps, finger appearance, professional movement cleanup and full audiovisual/phoneme review remain open. Source-paired mouth/collar stills improved, but no photographic likeness approval is claimed. CAC-2 and CAC-4 stay In Dev.
