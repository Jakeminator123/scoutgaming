## What is connected

The anatomical model includes independently deformable eyes, eyelids, oral surfaces and facial shapes. It retains the existing body, finger/contact rig and physical table. The corrected neck export is bound by SHA-256 in `web/public/dealers/astrid/rig/face-study/speech-manifest.json`; the model URL includes that revision so an old cached head cannot silently replace it.

All 17 Astrid script lines have measured facial frame data. The 15 indexed takes were measured from their shipped H264 streams; goodluck and win use their existing embedded audio/video takes. There are 1,756 source frames and no missing face detections. This is capture coverage, not proof that every phoneme looks natural.

`rigged-face-playback.mjs` uses the exact media element owned by shared speech playback. Embedded takes use video time directly; indexed takes use audio time plus the existing per-line delay. Preparation checks the line text, source URLs, delay and both audio/video hashes before allowing animation. Pause and seek therefore hold or resample the corresponding captured frame. Cancellation clears the face, and a stale completion cannot stop a newer utterance. No audio was replaced, re-timed or generated for this integration.

During silent dealing, the eyes follow the physical card using the current posed head's resting eye basis. The gaze eases back towards the player. This authored action gaze does not replace captured speech. The separate experimental speech-gaze studio is not imported into this game checkpoint.

The portrait camera frames the dealer, shoe and physical card area together. Controls shrink without scaling the actor independently. All occupied server hands keep their betting markers visible through the round, including a desktop hand resumed on a phone. Card size/readability and the overall phone composition still require further visual acceptance.
