Current source films one fixed landing plane. Actual second card CSS adds --ci1 offsets/rotation plus nth-child slot overrides. Simply removing first-card-only eligibility would deliver to the wrong geometric target.

Technically, the same authoritative DOM card could land on that fixed plane then animate into its fan slot: dealCardTo creates it and updates the issued shoeDraw once, and deliverCard awaits its existing promise. Identity, server counts and hidden-card stripping can be preserved without new API draws or actor warping. Prior card would be briefly covered at the common landing plane.

That post-release card movement would be unsupported by filmed hand motion. It is an interface arrangement, not a realism fix, and root explicitly did NOT approve implementation. No auto-fan or expanded eligibility was implemented. The first-card restriction remains.

To satisfy realistic second-card delivery, obtain a source with actual second/fan landing or a filmed arranging hand, then bind its observed release/landing geometry to the authoritative second card. Long hits and split hands need their own evidenced placement and collision handling. Existing end/start actor seam remains separate.
