# Edge-controlled rotation source review

All 73 native frames of both 24 fps clips reviewed; source-resolution detail sheets inspect narrowing/broadening. Full-body samples n0/24/48/72 check gross silhouette, sleeves and camera.

## Extract-edge

- n0–17: preparation at shoe; red card becomes visible at outlet about n18.
- n18–29: card emerges and is lifted upright by feeding hand.
- n30–49: receiving hand approaches/grips, with an unnecessarily long upright display.
- n50–64: red rectangle progressively narrows while the feeding hand releases and returns to shoe. The projected plane and edge change continuously, with receiving fingertips remaining attached. This is materially better than prior extract-turn broadside texture morph.
- n65–72: narrow edge reaches supplied endpoint.

## Edge-face

- n0–42: long edge-on hold and travel toward body centre. This wastes roughly 1.75 seconds before the turn completes.
- n43–48: edge/sliver progressively broadens into the face-up rectangle under the same receiving fingertips. The enlarged frames show changing projected width, rather than replacement of red texture on a continuously broad camera-facing plane. Conditional plausible rotation source; no clear stick-length growth was identified in this interval.
- n49–63: card is unnecessarily lifted/presented face-up.
- n64–72: lowered toward the low-contact endpoint.

## Verdict

**Conditional useful geometric-rotation experiment, still not an economical professional deal.** These explicit edge constraints improve visible paper rotation. I would not reject this pair solely as the old broadside texture morph. Finger attachment remains plausible and corrected sleeves remain long in reviewed crops; no gross body jump was seen in full-body samples. This is visual evidence, not a solved rigid-body reconstruction or subpixel contact calibration.

Do not claim full motion acceptance: both clips contain prolonged upright/edge-on holds and the reference turns compactly near the shoe. Do not cut out n50–64 or n43–48; those are the actual rotation evidence. Any cadence preview must retain those continuous intervals, show cross-clip seam and label retiming honestly. Neither clip alone forms a complete acceptable cycle; extraction, rotation, supported slide, release and return must be inspected together at the intended camera before gameplay integration.
