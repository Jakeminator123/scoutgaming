# Scene footage — real reference material for the 3D scene

Everything here is our own filmed/authored material already in the repo, unpacked into usable form. Nothing is generated, no credits were spent.

## plates/
- `room-plate-clean.png` — the empty room plate (no dealer). This is the background the hands matte is computed against, so it is the exact room behind Astrid, clean. Best single asset for a Blender backplate or for matching the wall and lighting.
- `astrid-room.jpg` — the room as shipped with her actor pack.
- `<dealer>-scene.png` / `<dealer>-table.png` — full scene and table art per dealer. Astrid is Dark Hall, Amira is Golden Salon, Mei is Azure Venue. Use these when the background floor gets its other tables: each room has its own palette.

## video/
Astrid's filmed performance takes, unpacked to plain 1920x1080 RGB (the originals are 1920x2160 with alpha masks stacked underneath, which is why they look wrong if opened directly).
- `astrid-centre-native.mp4` — deal to centre
- `astrid-dealer-front.mp4` — her own upcard
- `astrid-left-outlet.mp4` / `astrid-right-outlet.mp4` — deal left and right

Use these for motion timing, hand poses, how the card leaves the shoe, and how light moves over her while she reaches.

## frames/
Stills pulled from the above: front idle per dealer, three points of the centre deal, plus close-ups of the shoe, the hands on the felt, and the face lighting.

## What the reference actually tells us
One warm overhead bar is the only strong light. Everything past about two metres falls into darkness. The felt is dark blue-green and matte, not bright green. The shoe is white acrylic with a red stripe. Shallow depth of field, heavy vignette, filmic grade.
