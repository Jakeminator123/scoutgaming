Open `http://astridreach.localhost:4173/?dealer=astrid&performance=staged&reach=tracked`.

This is a video-deformation prototype, not seven newly filmed or contact-approved takes. The original three player clips remain the source. For each actual destination, their measured attached card and the filmed hand receive the same translation. A tessellated actor layer blends the displacement down the arm from an approximate fixed shoulder. Both actor and foreground-finger layers share the transform; the face and upper body stay outside the affected arm region. Pickup starts unchanged, and the displacement eases out by the original rest frame. Source bytes and calibrated source observations remain unchanged. The source calibration does not approve the transformed result.

The initial radial deformation caused obvious inner-seat forearm kinks and was rejected. The revised arm-segment weighting improved the inspected inner-right release. Tested a two-seat round at positions 3 and 5 (10 staked, 10 returned) and a complete seven-seat round (35 staked, 5 returned; balance 965 to 935), including two cards per seat, seven Stand actions, dealer resolution, collection and return to betting.
