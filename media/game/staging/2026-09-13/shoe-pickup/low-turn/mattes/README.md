## Sampled failures: raw masks are NOT production-ready

Reviewed7 chronological RGB/person/checkerboard samples per clip plus native-size hand crops (source frame36, slide frame60).

- Broad head, face and jacket remain in the mask across inspected samples; no missing whole actor or frame gap. This is sample review, not full temporal edge approval.
- Source frame36 red-backed card is partially retained as grey alpha. Person segmentation does not distinguish the held prop from fingers. Black/grey shoe mouth is also retained, with stray table-arc pixels. This would cause double shoe/prop ghosts over the live game.
- Slide frame60 retains a large grey shoe silhouette, printed face-up card, and table lettering beside the delivery sleeve. Lower torso/rail contamination appears in multiple samples. These are source RGB leaks, not merely an alpha edge that can be fixed by global erosion.
- Finger separations are softened by the coarse mask. There is no separate foreground-finger G channel yet. Removing paper with a broad white/skin threshold could delete fingertips. Need source-specific paper/room exclusion with explicit finger-preservation review at native extraction/roll/release frames.
- Do not promote full background or pack these masks as final R actor. Next step is measured prop/room subtraction and G finger masks; hold exact source-frame correspondence and keep native masks as immutable diagnostic inputs.
