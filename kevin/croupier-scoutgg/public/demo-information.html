Demo information

Your dealer is an AI

The dealer at this table is not a human. It is an AI agent with a computer-generated face and voice, and it is there for entertainment only. It does not decide or influence the cards, outcomes, payouts or your balance; those come from the game server’s shuffled shoe and fixed rules described below.

This demo uses virtual funds only. There are no deposits, withdrawals or real-money prizes. It is not presented as a certified real-money game.

Rules and returns

The server shuffles an eight-deck shoe, burns one card and reshuffles before the next round after 312 cards have been removed. Cards are dealt without replacement. The dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack when showing an ace or a ten-value card.

  • A natural blackjack pays 3:2; an ordinary win pays 1:1. A push returns the stake. A busted hand loses even if the dealer also busts.
  • Double is available on the first two cards. Splitting permits up to four hands per box, including ten-value pairs. Split aces receive one card per hand; split 21 pays 1:1. No surrender.
  • Insurance costs half the main stake and pays 2:1 if the dealer has blackjack. It is separate from the main hand.
  • Perfect Pairs uses your first two cards. Perfect pair 25:1, coloured pair 12:1, mixed pair 6:1.
  • 21+3 uses your first two cards and the dealer upcard. Suited trips 100:1, straight flush 40:1, trips 30:1, straight 10:1, flush 5:1. A-2-3 and Q-K-A are straights; K-A-2 is not.

“Pays X:1” describes profit. Total return includes the winning stake. Side bets are evaluated from the opening cards and credited once at round settlement. “Previous return” belongs to your last completed round, not the current hand. Your round receipt is shown under Hands & results.

Bets follow the table limits shown in the header: a minimum and maximum main bet per box, and a lower cap on side bets at Table Studio tables. Betting closes 20 seconds after the first bet or when everyone is ready. Decisions have 60 seconds after the dealer sequence; expiry stands the hand or declines insurance.

Take a break

The table’s Take a break button blocks new bets and refills for 24 hours across this browser profile’s tables. It survives refresh and server restart and cannot be cancelled early. Existing hands can stand or time out.

This demo control is tied to the browser’s session cookie. It is not identity-verified self-exclusion and can be bypassed by a different browser or clearing cookies. For help with gambling, visit Stödlinjen or your local support service.

Chat and data

Use a nickname. Your table chat is visible to other visitors with the same table link. Do not post personal, financial or identity details. The model receives the current message only, without automatic wallet balances, cards, player history or your display name. It can select a response from a fixed English response bank; it cannot author speech or change game outcomes. Obvious sensitive-data patterns are blocked, but that filter cannot identify every kind of personal information.

Current providers are Google Gemini for response selection, Cartesia for voice and RunPod for lip-sync processing. A table join creates a spoken greeting from your display name. For explicit factual game questions, the server reads limited public game state and the requesting player’s own hand to create a fixed factual reply; balances, other players’ hands and the dealer’s hidden card are excluded. The model receives only the current chat message and cannot author speech or change game outcomes. Prerecorded game clips play independently.

Recent chat is held in server memory. Generated reply videos use unpredictable URLs and stop being served after 24 hours; storage pruning runs when new replies are generated. Safety logs contain policy/model versions, response identifiers and content hashes, not raw chat or names, with a 30-day cleanup policy. Demo wallets, nicknames, requests and round receipts remain in the server database. Provider retention and the final account-data retention schedule still require operator review.
