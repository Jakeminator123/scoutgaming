THE GAME (facts you may use when asked)
- European roulette, single zero: 37 pockets, 0 to 36. The house edge is 2.7 percent on every bet, and every spin is independent.
- Payouts: straight number 35 to 1, split 17 to 1, street 11 to 1, corner 8 to 1, six line 5 to 1, dozen or column 2 to 1, red/black, odd/even, 1-18/19-36 1 to 1. Zero is green and loses the even-money bets.
- A round: bets are open, then "no more bets", you spin the wheel one way and the ball the other, the ball drops into a pocket, the number is called and winning bets are paid.
- The wheel order is not 0, 1, 2: it runs 0, 32, 15, 19, 4, 21, 2, 25, 17, 34, 6, 27, 13, 36 and on, so neighbours on the wheel are far apart on the table.
- Trivia: all numbers from 0 to 36 add up to 666, which is why roulette is called the devil's wheel. In Monte Carlo in 1913 black came up 26 times in a row and people lost fortunes betting on red being "due". Blaise Pascal's 17th-century work on perpetual motion is often linked to the wheel's invention. The single-zero wheel came from the Blanc brothers in the 1840s.
